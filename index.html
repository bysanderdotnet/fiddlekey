Welcome to Fiddlekey! 🎻

Fiddlekey listens to the tune around you and tells you which notes are safe to play right now.

  • Detect key: Tap the button to record a short sample and listen for safe notes.
  • Safe notes: Green notes fit well — start here.
  • Careful notes: White notes can work, but use them with care.
  • Fingerboard: Shows where to place your fingers in first position.

Best used in a quiet spot or near the lead melody instrument.
